# Supplier image licence scan, 20 brands

Card W23-04, scanned 2026-09-18. **No image was downloaded in this card**, by
instruction: this is a reading of what each manufacturer publishes about reusing its
pictures.

**Why it exists.** Mihai answered "Da" for all 20 brands
(`docs/client-answers/2026-09-18-supplier-checklist.md`). That was ruled to be a supply
claim, usable for catalogue listing, and **not a photo licence**. A manufacturer's
photograph may only enter this repo with its own provenance row under R-W, so each brand
needs a licence position of its own. This file is that position, brand by brand.

## How to read the verdict column

| Verdict | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Permitted, conditions** | the manufacturer publishes terms that allow a trade partner to use its images in the partner's own advertising, including a website. The licence URL is in the row, and the conditions are quoted |
| **Editorial or press only** | terms are published and they permit press or editorial use, which **this site is not**. A commercial site of a reseller falls outside them |
| **Reserved** | the published terms reserve all rights, or forbid reproduction beyond personal use |
| **No published licence found** | nothing published was found that grants or defines third-party image use. The URL column says what was checked |

**One brand of twenty permits what this site would need, and even that one is
conditional.** Everything else needs a written permission from the manufacturer or its
distributor before a single picture is committed.

## The one permitted case, in its own words

Caparol's media database terms, read on 2026-09-18:

- **Who:** "Only You as [a] registered user or persons belonging to the company you gave
  when registering."
- **What:** "Download of the files for use in your own advertising material in all
  advertising media, such as print media, electronic media and online media (for example
  your own websites and adverts on third-party websites)."
- **Limits:** use only "in connection with the promotion of Caparol products"; no
  modification of the files, beyond adding your own contact details or logo to an advert
  template; Caparol's copyright notices may not be altered or removed; the files may not
  be passed on, and never for payment.

**What that means for this site, plainly.** A Caparol image could be used on a Caparol
product card, if Rapid Construct registers, keeps the image unmodified and keeps the
copyright notice. **It is still not automatic**, and R-W still wants a row naming the
licence URL above.

## What this scan does not say

- It does **not** say Rapid Construct has a dealer account with any of these
  manufacturers. Q-SUPPLIERS ruled the client's answer to be a supply claim only, and no
  account is claimed anywhere on the site.
- It does **not** cover a distributor's own photographs, a photographer's work, or stock
  libraries. Those need their own licence and their own row.
- It is a reading of what was published on **2026-09-18**. A manufacturer can change its
  terms; a row here is evidence of that day, like every other dated record in this repo.

## The rule this leaves in place

**No manufacturer image is committed without a per-brand licence row under R-W.** Today
that means: nineteen brands need a written permission first, and the twentieth, Caparol,
needs registration and the conditions above.
