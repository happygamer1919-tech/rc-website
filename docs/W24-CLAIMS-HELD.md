# W24-CLAIMS-HELD.md

Every sentence the wave 24 mirror pages copied structure from but **did not render**,
with the page it came from and the position it occupied.

**Why this file exists.** Ruling W24-R6, verbatim:

> Imperlux pages: copy structure and text, replace "Imperlux" with "Rapid Construct".
> Any sentence asserting a company fact (own workshop, team, years, warranty, discount
> figure, counts, coverage, certificates) is NOT rendered. Write it to
> docs/W24-CLAIMS-HELD.md with page and position, leave the layout slot in place with
> neutral existing RC copy or empty. Owner ticks them later.

And W24-R7:

> Imperlux prices are not published. Slots render "Pret la cerere". GARD_FORBIDDEN stays
> as is. Reduceri tile reads "Reduceri" / RU equivalent, no percentage.

**How to read a row.** Each is a claim about a business, made on `imperlux.md`. It is
true of them, and this repo has no source saying it is true of Rapid Construct. Tick a
row and it can be written; leave it and the layout slot stays empty or keeps neutral copy
that is already Rapid Construct's.

**Nothing here is a product fact.** A model name, a profile description, a thickness, a
weight and a colour count are properties of a material and are rendered. A warranty
length, a delivery time, a price, a discount, a count of clients or showrooms or staff, a
certificate and a year in business are claims about a company and are held.
