# W26-07 · The three copertine images, from Unsplash and Pexels

Card W26-07. Branch `w26/w26-07-copertine-slots`, stacked on W26-06 (#129). PR only.

## Your ruling worked as written

W26-R8: "Download from Pexels or Unsplash page URLs directly." Last wave these sites refused
every request (307, 403, 401), but that was measured with a command-line tool. **A real browser
gets through**: both libraries' search pages and photo pages load, and their image servers hand
over the files. So **nothing needed the Google fallback**. Each picture was found by searching
the library, compared on contact sheets, and **its licence read on its own photo page** before
it was downloaded.

of our work. That is now a gate rule: the library origin is accepted **on these three slots
and nowhere else**. Watched fail on a product slot and pass on a copertine one.

Your review list (`docs/PHOTO-REVIEW-W25.md`) gets a **Copertine** section, with all three
flagged `library`, so you can check them.
