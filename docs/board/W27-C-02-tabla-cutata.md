# W27-C-02 · Tablă cutată gets its own group, and a ninth tile

Card W27-C-02. Branch `w27/w27-c-02-tabla-cutata`, stacked on W27-C-01.

## What you will see on the roofing page

- **A new filter chip, "Tablă cutată", with 7 products**: T-12, VP-20, PK-20, H-35, C-15, C-44 and
  H-60. They came out of "Accesorii de acoperiș", which drops from 53 to 46. The chips still add up:
  4 + 7 + 1 + 2 + 18 + 46 = 78 = Toate.
- **A "Compară modelele: Tablă cutată" table** with price, finish, quality tier and colour count.
- **A ninth tile**, a full-width strip under the four product tiles, opening the new section. Same in
  Russian ("Профнастил").

## Two things I decided, and why

1. **DRIPSTOP stays in Accesorii.** It is an anti-condensation coating for profiled sheet, not a sheet,
   and moving it would have put one product in two sections.
2. **The new tile is a wide strip on a third row**, so the four tiles above it do not move. Its picture
   is the T-12 packshot cropped by the tile, and it is the weakest picture on the page: the packshot is
   small and watermarked. **Q-W27-01 asks you for a real photograph** of a profiled-sheet roof; one
   landscape file from your folders replaces it.
