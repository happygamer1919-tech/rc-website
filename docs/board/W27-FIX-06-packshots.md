# W27-FIX-06 · Product pictures without Imperlux's mark

Card W27-FIX-06. Branch `w27/w27-fix-06-packshots`, stacked on W27-FIX-05.

## What this does

Your decision W27-R-13: the previews that carried Imperlux's orange mark are replaced by the
manufacturer's own packshot of the same model. Ten pictures change on the roofing page:

- **The seven metal tiles** (Barcelona, Madrid, Bavaria, Heta, Zet, Finn, Izi). Imperlux is the
  maker of these (its pages say so), so there is no other manufacturer to go to. Imperlux's own
  product pages carry a clean render of each sheet without the mark and without the printed name;
  those are what the cards show now. This is the "kept-imperlux" list: still Imperlux's pictures,
  because Imperlux makes the tiles, now without its branding.
- **IKO Cambridge and IKO Superglass Hex**: IKO's own colour swatches from iko.eu (Cambridge in 52
  Dual Black, Hex in 01 Black).
- **Creaton Rapido**: the maker's own render in dark brown. The factory that makes Rapido now sells
  as swissporTON (the swisspor group bought CREATON's south-east European works), so the picture
  comes from swissporton.ro; the card still says Creaton Rapido, as Imperlux does.

All 76 product pictures on the roofing, Novatik and fence pages were then looked at twice by
independent viewers: no other overlaid third-party mark. Four gutter parts carry a BILKA stamp
pressed into the metal (Imperlux's gutters are Bilka-made) and the membranes carry their factory
print; those are the products themselves and are left as they are.
