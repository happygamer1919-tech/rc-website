# W27-FIX-15 · Acoperișuri and Garduri in the Catalog

Card W27-FIX-15. Branch `w27/w27-fix-15-catalog-roof-fences`, from `main` after #166. Urgent, from
Mihai through you.

## What this does

- **Order**: the Catalog (the index page and the header menu) now starts with Sisteme de
  termoizolație, then Acoperișuri, then Garduri, then the rest as before.
- **Acoperișuri**: the Catalog entry opens a page with the same tiles the Acoperișuri service page
  shows (Țiglă metalică, Rocă vulcanică Novatik, Calculează prețul, Reduceri, then Țiglă ceramică,
  Șindrilă bituminoasă, Sisteme pluviale, Accesorii, Tablă cutată), and nothing else: no intro, no
  project pictures, no product list. Each tile goes where it goes on the service page: the tile page,
  the Novatik page, or the roofing products filtered to that section.
- **Garduri**: one product section, so the Catalog entry opens the fence models page directly.

## What to look at

`https://rapidconstruct.md/catalog/`: nine tiles, Acoperișuri second and Garduri third. Press
Acoperișuri: the tiles page; press Șindrilă bituminoasă there: the roofing products, filtered.
Press Garduri on the Catalog page: the fence models. The header's Catalog menu shows the same order.
