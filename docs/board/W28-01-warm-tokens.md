# W28-01 · Warm tokens, one motion curve, visible outlines, quieter orange

Card W28-01. Branch `w27/w28-01-warm-tokens`, stacked on W27-C-06. The first design card of the final
run; the numbers are the ones in your dispatch.

## What changed, in plain terms

- **Every grey is warmer**: text and the dark bands are one warm charcoal, secondary text a warm grey,
  lines a warm line. The orange is untouched.
- **Form fields and filter buttons have an edge you can see.**
- **Everything moves the same way**: one curve, hovers at 320ms, reveals at 380ms; buttons lift 1px.
- **Less orange**: the four orange top stripes are gone, and the promo stripe is a quiet warm ribbon
  with an orange dot.
- **Dark-band lines are hairlines**, not solid white tape. Supplier logos are more legible at rest.
- **No text under 14px** anywhere.
- **Clicking an anchor lands below the header** instead of under it.

## Two things kept as they were

The social-share image and the grey placeholder pictures keep their old charcoal and greys; they are
generated files and are not regenerated by a design card.
