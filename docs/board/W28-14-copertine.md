# W28-14 · Copertine: real photographs, a Catalog group, and the price that does not exist

Card W28-14. Branch `w28/w28-14-copertine`, stacked on W28-13 (#171). Under the wave 28 dispatch.

## What this card does

- **Every one of the twelve copertine models shows a real photograph from Imperlux's own model
  page** instead of the line drawing. All twelve models exist there (their IL301 to IL312 are your
  C-01 to C-12, in order). Three viewers looked at all 36 pictures Imperlux publishes; nine cards take
  the page's main photograph, three take a gallery picture that is better for a product card
  (badge-free for C-01 and C-02, the whole structure for C-10). No Imperlux logo or text is on any.
- **The copertine are in the Catalog**: a "Copertine" tile on the Catalog page and a row in the
  header's Catalog menu, both opening the copertine page, which now carries the group chip
  "Copertine 12" above the cards. A gate holds the twelve to the data.
- **Prices: none, and that is the source's doing.** Imperlux prints "Preț după măsurători" on every
  copertine page and explains in its FAQ that it will not show a per-square-metre figure. So each
  card asks for a price, exactly as your earlier rule for unpriced Imperlux products says, and the
  page's own line "prețul se stabilește după măsurare" stays true. The twelve models are listed in
  Q-W28-02; send figures and they land one per model.

## Two things to confirm (Q-W28-02)

- Seven photographs show a parked car with a readable maker badge (Mercedes, Mazda, Hyundai,
  Lexus). I read "no third-party mark on a product card" as being about a supplier's logo laid over
  the picture, not a car in the picture. If you disagree, three models (C-06, C-08, C-12) have no
  badge-free photograph at the source and would go back to drawings.
- Imperlux's pictures of C-01 look like renders rather than photographs; the badge-free one ships.
