# W28-27 · Turnkey examples: masonry instead of timber frame

The fourth dispatch (its "W28-25", an id already taken). Five of the ten stock pictures on the turnkey gallery show a North American timber-frame house; each is replaced in place by a masonry or brick house picture from the allowed set, viewed twice under the owner's words, with its manifest rows replaced and alt text in both locales. Gallery count stays 12; Lighthouse stays at or above 90.
