## What it was ruled to mean

Ruled at W23 (DECISIONS.md, "W23 rulings"), quoted here so the record carries its own
reading:

> The all-Da answer is Mihai's claim of supply per brand, usable for catalogue listing.
> It is NOT evidence of a manufacturer account and NOT a photo licence. Manufacturer
> imagery enters only with a per-brand licence row per R-W.

So, concretely:

1. A brand **may be named as a product's manufacturer** in a catalogue record.
2. **No manufacturer image may be committed** on the strength of this answer. The licence
   position per brand is `docs/SUPPLIER-LICENCE-SCAN.md`.
3. It says nothing about stock, availability, lead time, dealer status or an account, and
   none of those may appear on the site.
4. Dasterum, Imperlux and Fațade 3D remain refused **by name on the catalogue category
   pages** (W17-02). That prohibition is separate and this answer does not touch it.
