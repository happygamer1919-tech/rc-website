## W26-R2 · An audit of a third-party site reads the rendered DOM

> Audit standard, standing: any audit of a third-party site uses the rendered DOM in headless
> Chrome, not the HTML response. Wait for network idle. Record per page: sections in order, tile
> labels and image URLs, product cards with every field, comparison tables, and what each click
> opens. A finding of "does not exist" requires a rendered screenshot attached in the audit file.

**This exists because W25-24 got it wrong**, and the last clause is the part that would have
caught it: a screenshot of the top 4,000px of a long page is not evidence that something is not
on the page, and attaching one to the audit file is what makes that visible to a reader.

**"Wait for network idle" is a real instruction, not a flourish.** A `--virtual-time-budget`
that expires before the last request returns produces a DOM that is missing exactly the
late-rendered sections this rule is about.

**It is STANDING**, so it governs the imperlux audit this wave asks for and every audit after it.

## W26-R15 · No em dash and no en dash in anything this repo authors

> Dash gate: U+2014 and U+2013 are banned in every authored file (content, templates, scripts,
> board, rulings, commit messages). Replace with period, comma, colon, parentheses or hyphen.
> Exemptions only: third-party titles quoted verbatim in the audit file, and frozen records under
> R-S. The gate reads bytes, not a shell escape, and is proven on a planted defect.

**R-S freezes three files by name** (`docs/CLAUDE.md` section 17): `DECISIONS.md`,
`docs/QUESTIONS.md`, and the dated wave sections of `RELEASE-NOTES.md`, with the backlog's entry
bodies beside them. **Board cards are not among them**, so board cards are fixed.

**A frozen file is exempt only as it stands**: new text appended to `DECISIONS.md` or
`QUESTIONS.md` is authored today and is held to the ban. **Commit messages** are checked for the
commits a pull request adds; the published history cannot be rewritten without a force push,
which is a hard stop, so the fifteen dashes in nine old commits are reported and not touched.

**Two conflicts are recorded, not resolved quietly.** A ruling body may not have an existing
sentence edited (R-T), and R-Y carries two dashes in its own prose; they are struck and replaced
under R-R, which R-T permits. And `scripts/fixtures/styles-at-3392bb4.css` is a byte-pinned
historical snapshot that gate 22 verifies against git: editing it would break that gate, so it is
exempt as a quoted historical file, and the card says so.
