# RULING REGISTER W26-R · The wave 26 owner rulings, from 2026-09-22

**This file is the register of the `W26-R` ruling ids.** It continues
`docs/rulings/W25-R.md`, which holds `W25-R1` to `W25-R24` and is not renumbered.

**Why a new register rather than `W25-R25`.** The dispatch that issued these opens a new wave:
its cards are `W26-01` to `W26-07`. `W25-R.md` was itself created mid-wave, at the W25-07
dispatch, for exactly this reason. **If the owner meant `W25-R25` onward, the ids here map to
that sequence one for one and nothing else changes**; the numbers are unique either way and
every citation in the repo names the file it lives in.

Every ruling below is quoted exactly as the owner gave it. The prose under each one is this
terminal's reading and is open for ratification; the quote is not. **That distinction has
already been used once**: W25-R20's reading was struck and corrected at W25-27 because it had
narrowed the owner's words to nothing.

> **OVERTURNED: W25-R19 premise.** Owner has screenshots of imperlux.md /garduri/ and
> /acoperisuri/ 4-tile hub sections taken today. They render client-side; your HTML fetch

**The finding at W25-24 was wrong and the method is what was wrong with it.** That card

Neither reading could have found a section that renders in JS below the fold. **W26-R2 below
is the standing rule that comes out of it**, and it is this terminal's own error, not the
owner's.

models are copied for the first time by this wave.

**Q-W25-19 is answered: leave the warranty off.** The fence model cards ship with no warranty

## W26-R1 · The roof section takes the owner's own four photographs

> Owner override, roof section: the four files in /Users/ivan/fatade3d_04roof pics/ (folder
> name may contain a space; if not found, STOP and report the closest match, never guess)
> replace slots 01 to 04 in the Patru lucrari de acoperis section. Origin owner_supplied. Floor
> 450, no upscale, no edits. Caption stays neutral: no "realizate de noi" or equivalent, RO and
> RU.

**The folder was found with the space and nothing was guessed**: `/Users/ivan/fatade3d_04roof pics/`,
four files, each 900x675.

**"Origin owner_supplied" is R-W's client-supplied origin under another name**, and it is
recorded in R-W's own words rather than as a ninth origin string: source
`client direct transfer, <name>, DD.MM.YYYY`, licence
`owned by Rapid Construct, supplied for site use`. That is deliberate and it is the stricter
reading: gate 17 requires an image on that origin to carry **no metadata at all**, which a new
origin string would not have inherited.

**"No edits" decides the shape of the install.** The `.offer__media` box is `81 / 100`, portrait,
and these photographs are 4:3 landscape. The file is therefore installed **uncropped**, at its
native size for the `@2x` and scaled down for the `1x`, and the box crops it with the
`object-fit: cover` it has always used. Cutting a 0.81:1 file, which is what
`scripts/process-photos.js` did to the Unsplash pictures these replace, would have been an edit.

**The caption check is an assertion, not a read-through.** No `title` or `desc` on the four
cards may claim the work is ours, in either locale, and the four `alt` strings are rewritten to
describe the new photographs.

## W26-R2 · An audit of a third-party site reads the rendered DOM

> Audit standard, standing: any audit of a third-party site uses the rendered DOM in headless
> Chrome, not the HTML response. Wait for network idle. Record per page: sections in order, tile
> labels and image URLs, product cards with every field, comparison tables, and what each click
> opens. A finding of "does not exist" requires a rendered screenshot attached in the audit file.

**This exists because W25-24 got it wrong**, and the last clause is the part that would have
caught it: a screenshot of the top 4,000px of a long page is not evidence that something is not
on the page, and attaching one to the audit file is what makes that visible to a reader.

**"Wait for network idle" is a real instruction, not a flourish.** A `--virtual-time-budget`
that expires before the last request returns produces a DOM that is missing exactly the
late-rendered sections this rule is about.

**It is STANDING**, so it governs the imperlux audit this wave asks for and every audit after it.

**This closes Q-W25-18** and supersedes W25-R19's wording with a method that works: the file

will not hand over a file at the floor, **a screenshot of the rendered tile is permitted** at
`deviceScaleFactor: 2`, cropped to the tile.

**The override is still held to slot ids**, and these eight are the ids W25-R15 and W25-R19
already name, so the machinery that holds twelve and then sixteen does not widen.

## W26-R4 · A hub tile opens a page

> Tile links: a hub tile href must be a page URL, never a same-page anchor. Garduri tip jaluzele
> opens /servicii/modele-garduri/ (RU twin likewise). Fix gate 26 to reject anchors.

**This overturns gate 26's permission**, which was written at W25-24 on the reading that
W25-R24's "an href that answers 200" covered a resolving same-page anchor. It did, literally;
the owner has now said what the rule was for. **Gate 26 refuses an anchor by kind**, which is
what its first version did before it was loosened.

**Four tiles are affected**, all created by W25-24: `Reduceri` and `Prețuri și oferte` took
`#acoperisuri` and `#oferta` when they stopped being inert, in two locales. They need page URLs.

## W26-R5 · The roofing restructure

> Roofing restructure: Acoperisuri is removed from the catalog index; /catalog/materiale-acoperis/
> and its subpages stay as redirect pages to /servicii/acoperisuri/. The service hub keeps Tigla
> metalica, Roca vulcanica, Calculeaza pretul, Reduceri and adds a second 4-tile bento: Tigla
> ceramica, Sindrila bituminoasa, Sisteme pluviale, Accesorii de acoperis. Each product tile
> opens a section on the same page (filter bar retained) with product cards and a Compara
> modelele table. Imperlux products first; Dasterum products added only where the model name is
> absent; one card per model name, de la price = lowest grade, grades listed as variants in the
> card. Dasterum Sistem de scurgere merges into Sisteme pluviale. Profnastil, hidroizolatie,
> elemente suplimentare, siguranta, fixare go under Accesorii de acoperis. Price ruling
> unchanged: de la only, no strike-through, no percent, no countdown.

**Note the tension with W26-R4 and which way it resolves.** "Each product tile opens a section
on the same page" is a same-page anchor, and W26-R4 says a hub tile must open a page URL. They
are different tiles: W26-R4 governs the **hub** tiles at the top of the page, and this governs
the **product** tiles of the second bento, whose whole purpose is to move a visitor to a filtered
section below. Gate 26 must therefore be scoped to the hub bentos rather than to every
`.hub__tile`, or the second bento cannot exist.

**"One card per model name" is W25-R21 again**, applied to the roofing set rather than to the
tiles, and "Imperlux first, Dasterum only where the name is absent" is the merge order it
lacked.
