## R-W29-04 · Service gallery stock: nothing that reads as non-Moldova; the Garduri gallery's last seven go

> W29-04 Garduri gallery: remove non-Moldova stock, audit all galleries
> Ruling R-W29-04 (append to docs/rulings/W29-R.md): service gallery stock images must not show scenery that reads as non-Moldova. Hard criteria, remove if ANY applies: mountains or cliffs in frame, snow with non-local house style, Mediterranean or coastal vegetation, log cabins or timber houses, ornate wrought-iron gates on villas, visible foreign signage or plates, greenhouse or industrial campus backdrops. Close-up product shots (mesh, gabion, jaluzele panels, tile texture) with no scenery are allowed. No replacements from stock. Gaps stay until owner supplies RC-own photos.
>
> 1. Garduri gallery (RO and RU): remove the last 7 stock images: iron gate with flowered wall, snow gate with garage, road fence with greenhouse, mountain fence with orange chairs, gabion wall, chain-link mesh wall, log house with metal fence. Keep RC-own images and the three jaluzele close-ups. Delete files from public/, remove SOURCES.md rows, update alt maps.
> 2. Audit every other service gallery and the fatade service gallery against R-W29-04 criteria. Remove offenders, same cleanup. List per gallery in the report: kept count, removed files with the criterion that hit.
> 3. Gallery preview image rule from W29-03 still holds after removals, re-run gallery-preview-distinct.
>
> Acceptance: committed docs/reports/W29-04-REMOVED.md lists every removed path; named test gallery-removed asserts none of those paths is referenced in any page or manifest, exit 0; gallery-preview-distinct green; 320px gate green; screenshot of the Garduri gallery RO after change.

**What it amends.** R-W28-07 let stock pictures from R-W28-06's allowed set follow the owner's
photographs in every service gallery. This ruling keeps that and narrows it: a stock picture whose
frame reads as somewhere other than Moldova leaves its gallery, and the gap is not filled from stock.
The owner's own photographs are not in its scope and none is touched. W28-24's counts (115 stock
pictures, the eleven gallery sizes) are superseded by the counts in `docs/reports/W29-04-REMOVED.md`.

**Readings, open for ratification:**

- **"Service gallery"** is every gallery in `content/galleries.json`: the nine service pages, the
  copertine page and the fence gallery page (`/servicii/galerie-garduri/`), eleven in all, the set
  R-W28-07's reading named. **"Stock"** is `origin: "stock"` in that file.
- **The criteria are applied to what is in the frame, literally.** Where a word needed a reading:
  - **"timber houses"** is a building whose walls are timber boards or logs, a barn or shed included,
    and the interior of a stud-framed house stripped to its timber studs; timber-clad dormers on a
    stone house, and dark board cladding on a rendered modern house, are not.
  - **"visible foreign signage or plates"** is a sign carrying text, or a registration plate, in a
    non-Moldovan format, visible in the frame whether or not the text can be read. Language-free
    safety pictograms, painted grid numbers on a wall and the Russian title blocks on a drawing are
    not.
  - **"Mediterranean or coastal vegetation"** takes in palms, agaves and flowering plumbago, and is
    read to include an open sea horizon (coastal scenery), because Moldova has no coast.
  - **"greenhouse or industrial campus backdrops"** is a greenhouse, a business park or a factory
    estate behind the subject, including a backyard polytunnel and the factory sheds behind the
    industrial gallery's own steel frame.
- **The owner's Garduri list is followed as written.** Its seven are files 16 to 22 of the fence
  gallery, one for one with the owner's descriptions. Two of them, the gabion close-up (20) and the
  chain-link close-up (21), meet no criterion and are the kind of close-up the ruling allows; they go
  because the owner named them. The allowance governs the audit of the other galleries.
- **"The three jaluzele close-ups"** are read as the three stock pictures the list leaves, 13, 14 and
  15, because the gallery holds ten stock pictures and the owner removes seven. None of the three is a
  jaluzele (louvre) panel: 13 is a welded mesh panel, 14 a steel palisade, 15 an ornate cast-iron fence
  with star finials along a park lawn under trees. No criterion hits any of them, so all three stay.
  Asked as Q-W29-10.
- **"Gaps stay"** means no picture is added and no file is renumbered. A gallery whose preview
  photograph is removed takes R-W29-03's order: the first RC-own photograph, else the first stock
  photograph (the industrial gallery, whose preview 01 is removed, shows 02).
- **"Remove"** is the whole trace: the full and thumb files deleted from `public/`, the ledger entry
  and its alt text in both locales dropped from `content/galleries.json` (the alt map for a stock
  picture lives there), the `docs/images/SOURCES.md` row and both `docs/assets/PROVENANCE.md` rows
  deleted (R-W fails on a row for a file that does not exist). The source URL of each removed picture
  is kept in `docs/reports/W29-04-REMOVED.md`, which the named test reads.
- **Borderline pictures are removed when a criterion's words apply.** The ones a person may read the
  other way are named in Q-W29-11 with their criterion, so the owner can bring any back with one line.
