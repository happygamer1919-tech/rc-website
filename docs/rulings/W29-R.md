## R-W29-01 · The fatade3d.md catalogue pictures and structure may be reused; R-13 is scoped out of them

> R-W29-01 REVERSES R-W28-04 and R-W28-06. Owner Mihai obtained permission from Fatade 3D
> (fatade3d.md) on 28 Sept 2026 to reuse their catalog images and structure. R-13 (no third-party
> mark) is scoped to EXCLUDE catalog product images sourced from fatade3d.md. Manufacturer marks
> (Caparol, Phomi, etc) and Fatade packaging marks are allowed on catalog product images. R-13 still
> applies everywhere outside the catalog.

**The ids it names, corrected against the register.** In this register `R-W28-04` is "The phone hero
matches the desktop hero" (W28-22, #174), which has nothing to do with pictures. The fatade picture
ruling was *called* R-W28-04 by the second dispatch of wave 28 and is *recorded* as **R-W28-06**
("Fatade images: no mark of any kind, the maker's included"), because the id was taken (W28-R.md, the
second dispatch's premise 1). Both names in this ruling therefore point at the one recorded ruling,
**R-W28-06, which is what this reverses. The phone hero ruling R-W28-04 stands** and nothing in wave
29 touches the home page (W29-01 point 5). If the owner meant another ruling by "R-W28-06", that is
Q-W29-01 below; the default shipped is the reading here.

**What else it reaches, named so the amendments are exact:**

- **R-W28-10** narrowed R-W28-06 to "only fatade3d marks are excluded". This ruling allows the Fatade
  packaging marks on catalogue product pictures, so R-W28-10's exclusion is lifted for the
  catalogue; its other half (the manufacturer packshots stay) is unchanged.
- **W24-R2** ("no image, logo or asset from fatade3d.md enters the repo") and the R-W28-06 test rule
  "no fatade product image source host is fatade3d.md" are reversed **for catalogue pictures only**.
  `imperlux.md` and `dasterum.md` keep W24-R2 in full: the Acoperisuri and Garduri groups are not
  touched this wave.
- **W27-R-13** ("R-13" in the ruling) is scoped: no third-party mark on a product card still holds
  everywhere outside the catalogue (the Imperlux tile cards on `/servicii/tigla-metalica/`, the
  copertine models, the service pages), and inside the catalogue for any picture NOT sourced from
  fatade3d.md.
- **R-W28-06's generic-type reuse** (`reuse_of` a type photograph) is superseded for every product
  that receives its own fatade3d.md picture in W29-01; it survives only where W29-01's exceptions keep
  the current picture.

**Readings, open for ratification:**

## Test tooling: the dispatch names Playwright and axe; this repo has neither

**Recorded as a deviation, not ratified.** The acceptance lines of W29-01 to W29-03 name "playwright
test" and "axe". The repository has no `package.json`, no dependency and a written rule against adding
one without the owner (docs/CLAUDE.md section 1, the global rules). Every browser gate in `scripts/`
drives the runner's own Chrome over the DevTools protocol (`scripts/lib/cdp-ws.js`), zero dependency.
**Reading:** each named test is written as a zero-dependency CDP script in that shape, performing the
same clicks and assertions a Playwright test would, and exits non-zero on failure; **"axe passes" is read
as Lighthouse accessibility at 100 on the pages the card touches** (Lighthouse's accessibility category
is the axe-core rule set) plus a static check that no interactive element sits inside another. Adding
Playwright or axe-core as dependencies is Q-W29-03, default: not added.

## Motion: the fatade values and section 1

**Recorded ahead of W29-01, open for ratification.** W29-01 asks for fatade's measured transition values
reproduced. docs/CLAUDE.md section 1 caps motion at under 400ms and under 20px travel, opacity and
transform only, none under reduced motion. **Reading:** a measured value inside those limits is
reproduced as measured; a value outside them (a longer duration, a colour or size transition) is
reproduced at the nearest value the rule allows, and the measured and the shipped value are both
written in the report. A hover colour change (W29-02's border colour) is an instant state change, not an
animation.
